QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

What teams ask before they start.

What is AxoBoard?

AxoBoard is a live KPI dashboard and team-performance platform. It connects to your business systems, turns trusted data into clear dashboards, and distributes that performance to web, mobile, TVs, alerts, snapshots, and celebrations.

Is this a business-intelligence tool?

AxoBoard is designed for operational visibility and action, not open-ended analyst exploration. BI tools help analysts investigate what happened; AxoBoard helps teams see what is happening now and respond.

Do viewers need paid seats?

No. AxoBoard plans are designed around workspace capacity, editors, connected accounts, screens, refresh speed, and automation—not a tax on everyone who needs to see the score.

How fresh is the data?

Refresh speed depends on your plan and the source API. Every KPI shows freshness and connection health so “real time” is never a vague promise.

Can we use AxoBoard on office TVs?

Yes. AxoBoard includes full-screen display layouts, screen pairing, remote management, schedules, and dashboard loops as the TV product reaches production.

How does AxoBoard protect credentials?

Provider credentials stay server-side, connections are scoped to a workspace, and the product is designed around tenant isolation, revocation, encrypted secrets, visible audits, and minimum-access permissions.

What can we customize?

Dashboards, colors, logos, terminology, targets, alerts, celebration rules, sounds, and team competition presets can share one customer-owned theme.

Can I cancel after the trial?

Yes. The trial requires no payment card. Paid plans are designed to remain cancelable without surprise usage charges; contracted enterprise plans may have separate terms.
