# AxoBoard brand assets

## Primary logo

`axoboard-logo-low-poly.png` is the transparent primary mark: a forward-facing, low-poly leucistic axolotl with exactly three external gill stalks per side.

- Canvas: 1254×1254 RGBA PNG
- Intended use: app icon, navigation, splash screens and brand lockups
- Palette: warm white, pale blush, leucistic pink, coral pink and deep plum
- Minimum recommended display size: 32px

`axoboard-logo-low-poly-source.png` retains the flat chroma-key generation background. Keep it as the source artifact; do not use it in the product.

The next production step is manual vector tracing and optical cleanup into SVG. Do not auto-trace and ship without reviewing small-size geometry.

## Wordmark

The active AxoBoard wordmark is typeset in Fredoka 700 with `-0.03em` tracking. Pair it with the low-poly axolotl symbol in service navigation, sign-in, and marketing lockups.

`axoboard-wordmark-signature.svg` is a retired experiment retained only as a local design artifact. It is not wired into the product and must not be used in production or marketing.
